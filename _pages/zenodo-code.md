## HybridWind (MATLAB)

A hybrid profile–gradient approach for the estimation of surface fluxes.

- [Bitbucket](https://bitbucket.org/sukantabasu/hybridwind) · [Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/record/1998633)
- Reference: Basu, S. (2018). "A hybrid profile-gradient approach for the estimation of surface fluxes," *Boundary-Layer Meteorology* 170, 29–44.

## Thorpe $C_n^2$ (MATLAB)

Estimating refractive-index structure-parameter ($C_n^2$) profiles in the atmosphere from a Thorpe-length-scale approach.

- [Bitbucket](https://bitbucket.org/sukantabasu/thorpe-cn2/) · [Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/record/1998475)
- Reference: Basu, S. (2015). "A simple approach for estimating the refractive index structure parameter ($C_n^2$) profile in the atmosphere," *Optics Letters* 40, 4130–4133.
