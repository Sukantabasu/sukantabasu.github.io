I was a co-investigator on the **Deep Turbulence MURI** — a multi-university effort on the
wave optics of deep atmospheric turbulence: the underlying physics and its implications for
predictive modeling, mitigation, and exploitation.

- Project website: <https://sites.google.com/site/deepturbulencemuri/>
- MURI Technical Exchange Meeting hosted at the University of Miami, Florida (2013)

Related publications appear on the [atmospheric optics](/publications/optics/) page.
